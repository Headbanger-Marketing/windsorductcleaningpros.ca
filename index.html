FAQs

Before You Call, Check Here

If your question is not here, it is still worth a quick call.

Are your technicians certified and insured?

Yes, certified, trained, and fully insured on every job, and we will put that in writing. Ontario does not regulate duct cleaning as a trade, so certification, training, and insurance are the credentials worth demanding, and every job we run carries all three.

How often do Windsor homes need duct cleaning?

Every two to three years suits most of the city. Border-route dust, riverside humidity, pets, and renovations argue for the shorter end, and a one-time clean right after a reno is worth booking on its own.

How do I know the work was actually done?

Photos from inside your own ducts, shown before we start and again after we finish, before the van leaves. You should never have to settle for an invoice as evidence.

My dryer runs long. Is the machine dying?

Usually it is the vent, not the machine. A dryer that heats but needs repeat cycles is pushing air through packed lint. We clear the run to the hood and measure the flow, and if the reading says the appliance itself is fading, we will say so.

What happens during a whole-home cleaning, and how long does it take?

We connect the vacuum, pull and wash the registers, brush each supply and return run under suction, clean the blower compartment, and photograph the results. Count on three to four hours for a typical Windsor bungalow.

Can you get smells out of the ducts?

Yes, in sequence. Cleaning removes the residue holding the odour, then a sanitizer treatment on the clean duct walls addresses what lingers. Treatment without cleaning just perfumes dust, so we never sell it that way.

Why is the house dusty again right after cleaning it?

Return-side leaks are the usual culprit in this housing stock, quietly drawing basement and wall-cavity dust into the fan. If a cleaning fades within months, have the duct sealing looked at before paying for another pass.

Do you work outside the city?

Across Essex County, from LaSalle and Tecumseh out to Amherstburg, Lakeshore, Essex town, and Leamington. Same crews, same photo standard, same paperwork.

Do you serve areas outside of Windsor, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Windsor and nearby communities including LaSalle, Tecumseh, Lakeshore, Amherstburg, and the surrounding area.
